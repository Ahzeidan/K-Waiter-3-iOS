> يتطلب التطبيق حساب موظف صالحًا وسيرفر K-Waiter تابعًا للمؤسسة.

## Google Play — English

- Short description: `Restaurant orders, tables, payments, and printing on a waiter tablet`

Full description:

> K-Waiter is a professional restaurant operations app designed for tablets. It helps authorized staff create dine-in, takeaway, delivery, and pickup orders according to their role and device settings.
>
> Staff can find customers and manage delivery addresses, browse products and modifiers, handle combos, monitor tables and orders, record supported payment methods, route kitchen tickets, and print receipts. Order drafts, payment records, and print jobs can be safely queued during poor connectivity and synchronized when the connection returns.
>
> A valid staff account and an organization-managed K-Waiter server are required.

## App Review notes

- The app is restricted to authorized restaurant employees and requires a server URL plus staff credentials.
- Provide a dedicated review server, username, and password that remain valid throughout review.
- Suggested review flow: sign in, open Point of Sale, create a delivery order, select a demo customer and address, add a product, then save or pay.
- Printing needs a compatible printer but is not required to review the remaining workflow.
- The app has no in-app purchases. `ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption` is set to `false` because it only uses standard operating-system HTTPS encryption.

## Privacy declarations to review in each console

The app may process employee identifiers, customer name/phone/address, order and payment-method records, device identifiers, and diagnostics for app functionality, account management, fraud/duplicate prevention, and support. Data is encrypted in transit and is not used for advertising or cross-app tracking. Confirm the declaration against the production server and any third-party SDKs before submitting.
